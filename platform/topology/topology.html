F5 BIG-IP Next for Kubernetes 2.4 · open-source NVIDIA Run:ai

Lab topology

A tenant is defined once, as a Run:ai project. A small open-source controller reads it and builds that tenant's BNK front door: a Gateway with its own address, a rate limit sized from the project's GPU quota, an Endpoint Picker and token metering. Solid lines are inference traffic (data plane), dashed lines are configuration (control plane). Pick a flow to watch it travel, or click any box to see what it does.
